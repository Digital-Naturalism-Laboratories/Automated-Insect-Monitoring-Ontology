# Meeting with the Antenna / AMI team: terms to decide

Prepared 2026-11-19. Each item below is one decision. It gives the question, what we propose, what Antenna does today, and a blank line to fill in during the meeting. The items are ordered by how much they block moving data between Mothbox tools and Antenna. The full definitions are in the [README](../README.md), and the reasoning behind them is in [NOTES.md](../NOTES.md).

"Antenna today" was checked against Antenna's `main` branch as of 2026-11-13 (commit `be753663`).

## Background: why shared terms

Mothbox tools and Antenna will both publish to Darwin Core (GBIF) and may also publish to Camtrap DP. Internal names can differ as long as both sides map to the same published fields, so the [crosswalk table](../README.md#crosswalk) matters most. Shared words are still worth agreeing on, because people move between the two tools, and the words on screen shape how they think about their data.

---

## 1. One word for "what is this creature?"

**Decide:** Should machine and human answers share one word, told apart by a field saying which kind of thing made the answer?

**We propose:** Call both an **identification**, with `identifierType` (`machine` / `human`) and `identifierName` (e.g. `BioCLIP 2.5`, `A. Quitmeyer`). A machine identification also records the taxa list it used.

**Antenna today:** Antenna uses two models. `Classification` holds machine answers ("Machine prediction" on screen) and links to an `algorithm`. `Identification` holds human answers and links to a `user`.

**Prior art:** Both publishing standards already use one word with a type field. They just pick different words:
- Darwin Core: `identifiedBy`, `dateIdentified` (the Identification class), used for both people and machines.
- Camtrap DP: `classificationMethod` (`human` / `machine`), `classifiedBy`, `classificationProbability`.

So the real choices are (a) one word or two, and (b) if one, which word. "Identification" matches Darwin Core, which both groups export to.

## 2. What an identification is about: detection or occurrence

**Decide:** Can an identification point at a single detection, at an occurrence, or at either?

**We propose:** At either. Our [Identification](../README.md#identification) entry already says "a detection or occurrence".

**Antenna today:** Human identifications attach only to an `Occurrence`. Machine classifications attach to a `Detection`.

**Mothbox today:** Mothbot Classify attaches every identification to a detection (a "patch"). A Mothbot cluster often holds many individuals, so it can't simply become an occurrence.

**Why it matters:** When a human identification moves from Mothbox to Antenna, it needs an occurrence to land on. Without one, either each detection becomes its own occurrence, or the import must build occurrences from tracks.

## 3. Site, station and location

**Decide:** Will Antenna adopt "site" for named places, and keep coordinates separate from the site or station?

**We propose:**
- **Site** = a place a human has named (`siteName`, `siteDescription`), with no coordinates.
- **Location** = coordinates only, plus uncertainty and height above ground.
- **Deployment** = a device left at a location for a stretch of time.

We avoid "station" because it suggests a fixed spot, and Mothbox devices move often.

**Antenna today:** Antenna has both. `Site` ("Research Site" in code, "Site" on screen) groups deployments. `Deployment` is shown on screen as "Station" and carries `latitude` and `longitude`. So in Antenna, coordinates already belong to the station/deployment, not the site, which is close to our proposal. The question is mostly the word "Station" on screen.

**Options:**
- (a) Antenna renames "Station" to "Deployment" on screen.
- (b) Antenna keeps "Station" on screen, and we document it as Antenna's name for a deployment.
- (c) Something else.

## 4. Device type and individual devices

**Decide:** Should Antenna record individual devices (`deviceID`, e.g. `SnappySloth`) as well as device types?

**We propose:** `deviceType` (e.g. `MothboxPro`), `deviceID` (one physical unit) and `sensorType` (e.g. `camera`). We avoid "camera" for devices, because future ones may use lidar, radar or other sensors.

**Antenna today:** `Device` ("Device Configuration" in code, "Device type" on screen) is a kind of hardware, and Antenna has no record of an individual unit. The Oct 29 change (#1561) added filtering captures and sessions by device type and site.

**Why it matters:** Mothbox devices move between sites. Tracking an individual unit's calibration, firmware or faults needs a `deviceID`. Camtrap DP has the same split: `cameraID` (individual unit) and `cameraModel` (type).

## 5. Taxon keys and taxa lists

**Decide:** (a) Will Antenna store Catalogue of Life keys? (b) Will taxa lists record where they came from?

**We propose:**
- Store `col_taxon_key` beside `gbif_taxon_key`, and always send the taxon name and rank with any key.
- A taxa list records its source and DOI (e.g. a GBIF download DOI), so a machine identification can cite the exact list it was limited to.

**Antenna today:**
- `Taxon` has `gbif_taxon_key`, `bold_taxon_bin`, `inat_taxon_id` and `fieldguide_id`, but no Catalogue of Life key.
- `TaxaList` has only `name`, `description` and its taxa. It has no source, DOI or date.
- The draft Darwin Core export branch (`feat/dwca-export`) writes `taxonID` from `gbif_taxon_key`.

**Why it matters:** Newer GBIF species-list downloads use Catalogue of Life keys. Mothbot's `taxonID` has held Catalogue of Life keys since October 2026, so the two systems can't match taxa by key today.

## 6. Sessions and the 2-hour gap

**Decide:** Confirm that sessions and capture windows nest, and how Antenna should group Mothbox nights.

**We propose:** Events nest: deployment → session (about one night) → capture window (one continuous run of the attractor) → source file. See [What "event" means elsewhere](../NOTES.md#what-event-means-elsewhere).

**Antenna today:** Antenna starts a new session after a gap longer than `session_time_gap_seconds`, which defaults to 2 hours (set per project). The default Mothbox schedule has a **121-minute** break, from 23:59 to 02:00. So Antenna would split a default Mothbox night into two sessions, unless the project's gap is raised.

**Ask:** Should Mothbox projects on Antenna use a longer gap (e.g. 3 hours)? Or should the import mark nights explicitly, for example from the folder name, instead of inferring them from gaps?

## 7. Field-name style

**Decide:** camelCase or snake_case for shared field names?

- camelCase: Darwin Core and Camtrap DP (`deviceID`, `siteName`, `parentEventID`).
- snake_case: Antenna's code (`gbif_taxon_key`, `research_site`) and Python generally.

**Suggestion:** Use camelCase in the ontology and in exchange files, since that is what gets published, and let each codebase keep its own style internally.

## 8. Rotated boxes

**Decide:** Will Antenna accept an optional rotated box on a detection?

**We propose (not yet final on our side):** Every detection always has an upright box that every tool can read. When there is a rotated box, it is added as four corner points, with any angle treated as a convenience copy. See [Ways to write a box](../NOTES.md#ways-to-write-a-box).

**Antenna today:** `Detection.bbox` is a JSON field holding upright corners `x1, y1, x2, y2` in pixels.

**Decision:** ______________________________

## 9. Non-target labels

**Decide:** Is there a shared way to mark detections that aren't creatures of interest (blur, frass, debris, plant material, error)?

**Mothbox today:** Mothbot Classify has error labels (e.g. `ERROR_Blur`).

**Antenna today:** Antenna has a moth / non-moth classifier, and taxon `Tag`s.

**Prior art:** Camtrap DP's `observationType` has `blank`, `unknown` and `unclassified` alongside `animal`.

## Terms still open on our side

These can be raised if there is time, or used to ask how Antenna thinks about them.

| Term | Status | Question |
|---|---|---|
| [Target](../README.md#target) | Draft | "Target" or "stage" for the sheet, tube or flower creatures land on? Antenna has no equivalent. |
| [Source File](../README.md#source-file) | Draft | Antenna says "Capture" on screen and `SourceImage` in code. Does "source file" (which also covers video and audio) work for them? Antenna's `Detection.frame_num` suggests they already plan for video. |
| [Protocol](../README.md#protocol) | Draft | Antenna's draft export writes `samplingProtocol` as the fixed text "automated light trap with camera". Should protocol be a structured record (attractor, schedule, device) instead? |
| Project vs dataset | Open | Antenna's `Project` is the body of work: people, settings, default filters, taxa lists. Do we also need a separate "dataset" for a portable package of files? |
| Capture set | Not defined | Antenna's `SourceImageCollection` ("Capture set" on screen): a chosen subset of captures, used for processing or review. Do we need this term? |
| Algorithm / pipeline | Not defined | Antenna has `Algorithm` (one model) and `Pipeline` (a chain of algorithms). Our `identifierName` / `creatorName` would hold an algorithm's name and version. Worth defining both? |
| Withdrawn identification | Not defined | Antenna lets a person withdraw their identification (`withdrawn`) instead of deleting it. Should our Identification have the same attribute? |
