# Automated-Insect-Monitoring-Ontology
Working towards a data standard or data schema for devices that try to image arthropods automatically in nature by creating a shared vocabulary.


The reasoning behind each decision, the questions still open, and what we want to ask other groups (such as the Antenna / AMI team) are kept in [NOTES.md](NOTES.md). Agendas for meetings with other groups are in [meetings/](meetings/).

## How to read this glossary

Each term has a **Status**:

- **Decided**: agreed for this ontology and used by the Mothbox tools.
- **Decided, pending Antenna**: agreed here; to be put to the Antenna / AMI team, who may adopt it or suggest a change.
- **Draft**: earlier wording that has not been reviewed yet.
- **Open**: still under discussion (see [NOTES.md](NOTES.md)).

**Also called** lists the names other tools use for the same thing, so data can be translated between them.

## Glossary of terms

### Creatures and records

#### Creature
- **Status:** Decided
- **Definition:** Abstract term for a living thing that is of interest for this work.
- **Explanation:** Could be an insect, an arthropod, or another small animal.
- **Example:** gecko, beetle, bat

#### Occurrence
- **Status:** Decided
- **Definition:** One unique individual creature in the world.
- **Explanation:** An individual creature that occurs in a specific time and place. It can be seen in one detection or in many.
- **Example:** Jim the gecko, Sally the beetle, Lee the bat
- **Also called:** Occurrence (Antenna, Darwin Core)

#### Track
- **Status:** Decided
- **Definition:** A series of detections of one specific occurrence, linked across time.
- **Explanation:** Every detection in a track shows the same individual, not only the same kind of creature. Detections that only look alike form a [Cluster](#cluster).
- **Example:** a beetle walking over a screen, a bumble bee visiting close by flowers
- **Attributes:**
    - duration
- **Also called:** sequence (AMI Data Companion app); temporal sub-cluster (older Mothbot data)

#### Cluster
- **Status:** Decided
- **Definition:** A group of detections grouped by a common feature, such as how they look.
- **Explanation:** The detections in a cluster are not necessarily the same occurrence. A cluster often holds many individuals of one kind of creature seen over a night.
- **Example:** all the detections of small brown moths that a visual-similarity model puts together
- **Attributes:**
    - the feature or method used to group them (e.g. DINOv2 visual similarity)

### Evidence and identifications

#### Source File
- **Status:** Draft
- **Definition:** A media file that can have different types of backgrounds and may contain one or more target species.
- **Explanation:** any computer readable file format generated by the machine that might contain unprocessed detections
- **Example:** photograph, video, or audio track (or combined).
- **Assumptions:**
	- Static device.
- **Also called:** Capture (Antenna); source image, raw photo (Mothbot)

#### Detection
- **Status:** Decided
- **Definition:** Evidence from a source file of (ideally) one specific creature.
- **Explanation:** A detection can be created by a machine (a detection model) or by a human (for example by drawing a box). It is usually a region of a source file, such as a box in a photo or a stretch of time in an audio file, together with its cropped image or clip.
- **Example:** a box around a moth in a photo, and the cropped image of that moth
- **Attributes:**
	- Date and time stamp
	- Source file name
	- Information on bounding boxes (e.g. X & Y coordinates + dimensions) or start timestamp + length
	- Source image coordinates (center and bounding box)
	- `creatorType`: `machine` or `human`
	- `creatorName`: the specific model or person that made the detection (e.g. `Mothbot Detect 1.2`, `A. Quitmeyer`)
	- detection score, when a machine made it (see [Score](#score))
	- its identifications (see [Identification](#identification))
- **Also called:** Detection (Antenna); patch, shape, bot detection, human detection (Mothbot)

#### Identification
- **Status:** Decided, pending Antenna
- **Definition:** A claim about what taxon a detection or occurrence is.
- **Explanation:** Machines and humans both make identifications. Rather than using a different word for each (such as “prediction” for machines), every identification records what kind of thing made it and exactly who or what that was. Identifications can be used in training data sets or while and after validating AI results. This entry replaces the earlier term “Annotation”.
- **Example:** a machine identification of *Apis* sp. made by BioCLIP 2.5 using a taxa list with DOI 10.15468/dl.4j8kfb; a human identification of *Apis mellifera* made by A. Quitmeyer
- **Attributes:**
    - taxon: name and rank, with taxon keys when known (see [Taxon Keys](#taxon-keys))
    - `identifierType`: `machine` or `human`
    - `identifierName`: the specific model or person (e.g. `BioCLIP 2.5`, `A. Quitmeyer`)
    - taxa list used, with its DOI (see [Taxa List](#taxa-list)); this is the reference catalogue that avoids synonyms
    - identification score, when a machine made it (see [Score](#score))
    - date and time it was made
- **Also called:** Classification or Machine prediction (Antenna, for machine identifications); Identification (Antenna, for human identifications); classification (Mothbot Classify)

#### Agree
- **Status:** Decided
- **Definition:** An identification that endorses an existing identification.
- **Explanation:** When a reviewer confirms a machine's or another person's answer, the agreement is recorded as a new identification that points to the one it agrees with, so the record shows who checked what.
- **Example:** A. Quitmeyer agrees with BioCLIP 2.5's identification of a detection as Noctuidae.
- **Also called:** Agree (Antenna); Accept (older Mothbot Classify)

#### Determination
- **Status:** Decided
- **Definition:** The answer a record currently carries.
- **Explanation:** A detection or occurrence can collect many identifications over time. Its determination is the one that currently stands, usually the latest human identification, otherwise the best machine identification.
- **Example:** an occurrence with a BioCLIP identification of Erebidae and a later human identification of *Hypena scabra* has the determination *Hypena scabra*.
- **Also called:** Determination (Antenna); current classification (Mothbot Classify)

#### Score
- **Status:** Decided
- **Definition:** A number from 0 to 1 that a machine gives for how sure it is.
- **Explanation:** Always say what the score is about: a **detection score** (how sure a model is that a detection holds a creature) or an **identification score** (how sure it is of a taxon). Use “score” rather than “confidence”.
- **Example:** detection score 0.91; identification score 0.62
- **Also called:** confidence (older Mothbot data: `confidence_detection`, `confidence_ID`); score (Antenna)

#### Non-target Label
- **Status:** Decided
- **Definition:** A label applied to a detection we are not interested in.
- **Explanation:** It marks detections that are not creatures of interest or cannot be used, so they can be left out of counts and identifications without being deleted.
- **Example:** a blurry detection labelled as blur; a detection of insect droppings labelled as frass
- **Attributes:**
    - subcategory, commonly: blur, frass, debris, plant material, error
- **Also called:** error label (Mothbot Classify, e.g. `ERROR_Blur`); non-moth (Antenna's moth / non-moth classifier)

### Taxonomy

#### Taxa List
- **Status:** Decided
- **Definition:** A list of taxa that limits or describes which identifications are possible.
- **Explanation:** A taxa list can hold taxa of any rank: species, families, orders, or a mix. It is used to restrict what a model can answer and to record which reference list an identification was made against.
- **Example:** the insects of Panama and Costa Rica downloaded from GBIF; a list of the moth families expected at a site
- **Attributes:**
    - source and DOI (e.g. a GBIF download)
    - date created
- **Also called:** Taxa list (Antenna); species list (older Mothbot)

#### Species List
- **Status:** Decided
- **Definition:** A taxa list whose entries are species.
- **Explanation:** GBIF calls its checklist downloads “species lists”, which is where the Mothbox tools got the name. Use [Taxa List](#taxa-list) as the general term.
- **Example:** a GBIF species list download for Insecta in Panama

#### Morphospecies
- **Status:** Decided
- **Definition:** A group of creatures sharing certain features, judged to be the same type of thing.
- **Explanation:** Usually made by a person organizing creatures whose exact designations are not known, but which seem to be the same sort of thing. It is often a placeholder until the official species can be found. The same idea works at other ranks, such as a morphogenus or a morphofamily.
- **Example:** “brown moth with white kidney spot A”, used for every detection of that kind until it is identified to species
- **Attributes:**
    - rank it stands in for (morphospecies, morphogenus, morphofamily, ...)
- **Also called:** morphospecies (Mothbot Classify); unknown species (an unmerged Antenna branch)

#### Taxon Keys
- **Status:** Decided, pending Antenna
- **Definition:** Identifiers that tie a taxon name to an entry in a published checklist.
- **Explanation:** Different checklists use different keys, so each is kept in its own field. Always send the taxon name and rank as well, because a key from one checklist cannot be looked up in another.
- **Attributes:**
    - `gbif_taxon_key`: the GBIF Backbone Taxonomy key (a whole number)
    - `col_taxon_key`: the Catalogue of Life key (letters and digits, e.g. `B6L67`), which newer GBIF species list downloads use
- **Also called:** `gbif_taxon_key` (Antenna, which also stores `bold_taxon_bin` and `inat_taxon_id`, but no Catalogue of Life key); `taxonID` (Mothbot, holding a Catalogue of Life key since October 2026; Darwin Core)

### Places and time

#### Location
- **Status:** Decided, pending Antenna
- **Definition:** The actual GPS coordinates of where a thing is.
- **Explanation:** A location is only coordinates, kept separate from any name for the place, because Mothbox devices move often and the same named site can have devices at several exact spots.
- **Example:** 5.094, -80.34
- **Attributes:**
    - latitude and longitude
    - coordinate uncertainty, if known
    - height above ground, if known
- **Also called:** Station latitude and longitude (Antenna); latitude and longitude on the Mothbox field sheet. Replaces the earlier entry “Device Location”.

#### Site
- **Status:** Decided, pending Antenna
- **Definition:** A place that a human has given a name, where one or more devices are deployed.
- **Explanation:** A site is identified by its name and an optional description, not by coordinates (those are the [Location](#location)). Automated Insect Monitoring Symposium participants indicated a site is likely a zone 10–100 meters wide. We do not use “station”, because it suggests a fixed spot and Mothbox devices move often.
- **Example:** `siteName`: Les Beach Palm; `siteDescription`: creek near the big tree
- **Attributes:**
    - `siteName`: the name a human gives to a specific place
    - `siteDescription`: a brief optional description (e.g. creek near big tree, retired farmland)
    - Canopy height
    - Habitat type
- **Also called:** Site and Station (Antenna)

#### Deployment
- **Status:** Decided
- **Definition:** A collection of sessions.
- **Explanation:** Starts when a device is left in a location and ends when the device is removed from that location.
- **Example:** A season
- **Also called:** Deployment (Antenna code, Camtrap DP); Station (Antenna screens)

#### Session
- **Status:** Decided
- **Definition:** The source files from one observation period.
- **Explanation:** A session is an event inside a deployment, and it holds one or more [capture windows](#capture-window). Events nest in this order: deployment, session, capture window, source file. Darwin Core links nested events with `parentEventID`.
- **Example:** Source files captured during one night (dusk to dawn), one day (afternoon) or only during dusk
- **Also called:** Session or Event (Antenna, roughly one night); night (Mothbot); Event (Darwin Core). See [What “event” means elsewhere](NOTES.md#what-event-means-elsewhere).

#### Capture Window
- **Status:** Decided
- **Definition:** A continuous series of source file captures made while the attractor was running.
- **Explanation:** A capture window is an event inside a session. A Mothbox session usually has several capture windows, because the device runs its attractor on a schedule (by default five stretches of 59 minutes a night). An Antenna session usually has just one, because AMI traps run all night. Both fit the same nesting, so the data stays compatible.
- **Example:** photos taken every minute from 19:00 to 19:59
- **Attributes:**
    - start and end time
    - capture interval (time between source files)
- **Also called:** session (older Mothbox firmware settings)

### Hardware

#### Device
- **Status:** Decided, pending Antenna
- **Definition:** A machine that performs some kind of automated observation task.
- **Explanation:** Hardware that is deployed in the location. We avoid the word “camera” for devices, because future devices may sense in other ways (lidar, radar or other sensors).
- **Example:** all great devices listed in [InsectAI hardware-db](https://github.com/InsectAI-COST-Action/hardware-db)
- **Attributes:**
	- `deviceID`: the specific name of one unique device (e.g. `SnappySloth`)
	- `deviceType`: what kind of device it is (e.g. `MothboxPro`)
	- `sensorType`: what kind of sensor it uses (e.g. `camera`)
	- Sensor details, such as camera + lens or/and microphone used
	- Orientation (vertical vs horizontal)
	- Effective resolution (e.g. px/mm, kbits/sec)
	- Sensitivity range (IR, RGB, UV, infrasonic, ultrasonic)
- **Also called:** Device type (Antenna, for `deviceType`; Antenna has no record of `deviceID`)

#### Target
- **Status:** Draft
- **Definition:** The part of a device being directly observed where insects are intended to go for observation.
- **Explanation:**
- **Example:** a target sheet, a tube, or an artificial flower.
- **Note:** The name may change; “stage” is one option being considered.

#### Attractor
- **Status:** Decided
- **Definition:** specific stimulus used to lure creatures.
- **Explanation:** This might not be visible/ audible in the Source files.
- **Example:** chemical (smell), visual (UV, false flowers), or auditory (mating call).

### Methods

#### Protocol
- **Status:** Draft
- **Definition:** The method in which a task was carried out.
- **Explanation:** The description needs to include all necessary information to deploy the device in the same maner for a follow up survey.
- **Example:** checklist, bullet points, might include photodocumentary. Can be applied to a deployment, session, capture, or detection.

### Terms without definition (yet)
These might help to describe or distinguish things:
- appearance
- localisation
- record
- project and dataset (being considered)
- capture set: a chosen subset of source files (Antenna's “Capture set”)
- algorithm and pipeline: one model, and a chain of models (Antenna)
